What time is check-in and check-out?

Check-in is from 2:00 PM and check-out is by 10:00 AM. Need to arrive early or leave late? Ask us and we will do our best.

How do I book a room?

Fill in the form below with your dates, or call us. We will confirm your booking as soon as we can.

Is there parking and Wi-Fi?

Yes. Parking and Wi-Fi are available to all guests.

Do you serve meals?

Yes. Breakfast, lunch and dinner are served daily. Tell us about any food needs when you book.
